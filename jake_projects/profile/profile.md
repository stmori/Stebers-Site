I'm a Computer Vision M.S. student at the University of Central Florida (B.S. Computer Science, 2026). I like building vision systems end to end: fine-tuning and evaluating models on GPU clusters, then shipping them as real, containerized applications people can use.

My work spans **medical imaging** (nuclei segmentation, virtual staining, multimodal question answering on scans), **3D and geospatial vision** (cross-view geo-localization, Gaussian splatting), and **robotics** (a lunar-rover motor drive for the Florida Space Institute and an autonomous quadcopter stack). I care about the engineering around models as much as the models themselves: reproducible experiments, efficient fine-tuning, and infrastructure that doesn't fall over.
